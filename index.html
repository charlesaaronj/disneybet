🎢 How to play Who Said Diz?

Who Said Diz? is an engageing guessing game that turns queue lines into family game nights!

How to play:

  1. Add players (at least 3).
  2. Pick the attraction you’re waiting for.
  3. Answer an atttraction question in secret.
  4. Wager on who gave the selected answer.

How to win: Have the highest point total after all rounds and bonuses are scored. The winner gets a theme park snack🍿of their choice.

Summary

🏅 Bonus Summary

At the end of the game, these fixed bonuses are added to the final score:

🗺️
Top Land Collector — +3
Player with the most unique lands collected. Ties all earn it.
🎢
Top Attraction Collector — +3
Player with the most unique attractions collected. Ties all earn it.
🧠
Best Guesser — +2
Player with the most correct guesses. Ties all earn it.
🎲
Most Risky Player — +2
Player who risked the most total points during the game. Ties all earn it.

House bonus reminder: extra round points are only awarded if they divide evenly among all correct guessers.
