🎢 How to play Who Said Diz?

Who Said Diz? turns wait times into game time with a fast, funny guessing game for the whole family.

How it works:

Start by adding 3+ players, picking your attraction, and answering a question in secret. Then guess who said what and place your bets. Get it right? Score points.

Play all day—from your first ride to your last—collecting attractions and lands along the way for bonus points.

Win by ending the day with the most points… and claim a prize the family agrees on (snack, souvenir, or bragging rights)

💰 How Scoring Works

Every player starts with 10 points. Points are won and lost each round based on your guess and wager.

✅
Correct guess
You earn your wager amount back. Wager 3, gain 3.
❌
Wrong guess
You lose your wager amount. Wager 3, lose 3.
✍️
Author bonus
If you wrote the selected answer, you earn +1 point for every player who guessed wrong.
🏠
House bonus
An optional bonus added by the host. It's split evenly among correct guessers — only awarded if it divides evenly.
🛡️
Minimum score
No player can drop below 3 points, even after a bad round.

End-of-game bonuses are separate. Tap How are bonuses calculated? on the scores screen to learn more.

🎲 Wagers & House Bonus

Each round, you choose who wrote the answer and how many points to wager. Correct guesses win your wager back; wrong guesses lose it.

✅
Correct guess
You earn your wager amount. Wager 3, gain 3.
❌
Wrong guess
You lose your wager amount. Wager 3, lose 3.
👻
Ghost guess
Select Ghost if you think no one wrote this answer this round. If the answer is really a Ghost answer, Ghost guesses count as correct.
✍️
Author bonus
If you wrote the selected answer, you earn +1 point for every player who guessed wrong (except on Ghost rounds).
🏠
House bonus
An optional extra pot added by the host. If at least one player guesses correctly and the bonus divides evenly, those correct guessers split it.
