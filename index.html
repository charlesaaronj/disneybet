How to play!

Who Said Diz is a competitive social guessing game for theme‑park regulars, turning repeat ride lines into family game time.

In each line, everyone secretly answers a fun park question, then tries to guess who said it and wagers points on their hunch.

Guess right to win points; guess wrong and the person who said it gains points instead.

The player with the most points at day’s end wins an in-park 🍿 snack.

Not affiliated with The Walt Disney Company or any theme park.

About the app
What is Who Said Diz?
Who Said Diz? is a simple, family-friendly guessing and betting game designed to be played together on one phone while you wait in theme-park-style lines.
Is this an official Disney or theme park app?
No. This is an independent fan-made game. It is not affiliated with, sponsored, authorized, or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company or any other theme park or entertainment company.
Is this just for one phone?
Yes. The game is built for a single "family phone." Everyone takes turns looking at the screen and making their guesses and wagers on that device.
Is this a bet app?
No. There is no real-money bets in this game. Any "bets" are just for fun and whatever prizes your group chooses.
How can I support Who Said Diz?
If your family has fun using this app, why not buy us a coffee?
Privacy & data
What personal information do you collect?
The game is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. Player names and scores are stored locally on your device so your game can continue throughout the day.
Where is my game data stored?
Your in-game data (players, scores, settings) is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It stays on your phone and does not automatically sync to other devices.
Do you use cookies or tracking?
The core game does not need cookies to work. If optional analytics or improvements are added in the future, you will see a clear notice before any non-essential tracking is used.

💰 How Scoring Works

Every player starts with 10 points. Points are won and lost each round based on your guess and wager.

✅
Correct guess
You earn points from the pot. When multiple players are correct, they split the pot proportional to their wager.
❌
Wrong guess
You lose the points you wagered.
✍️
Author
If nobody guesses your answer, you win the whole pot. If you guess yourself (or your ghost answer), you are not included in any payouts.
🍯
Hunny Pot
Hunny Pot = Base points (1 pt per players) + wagers + hot round bonus - rounded up as needed to split evenly.
👻
Ghost
Some rounds include a Ghost answer. Guess Ghost correctly for 2 bonus points. If no one does, the Ghost author gets 2.
🛡️
Minimum score
No player can drop below 1 point.

End-of-game bonuses are separate. Tap How are bonuses calculated? on the scores screen to learn more.
