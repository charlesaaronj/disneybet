About the app
What is Who Said Diz?
Who Said Diz? is a simple, family-friendly guessing and betting game designed to be played together on one phone while you wait in theme-park-style lines.
Is this an official Disney or theme park app?
No. This is an independent fan-made game. It is not affiliated with, sponsored, authorized, or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company or any other theme park or entertainment company.
Is this just for one phone?
Yes. The game is built for a single "family phone." Everyone takes turns looking at the screen and making their guesses and wagers on that device.
Is this a gambling app?
No. There is no real-money gambling in this game. Any "bets" are just for fun and whatever prizes your group chooses, like snacks or bragging rights.
Privacy & data
What personal information do you collect?
The game is designed to collect as little personal information as possible. Player names and scores are stored locally on your device so your game can continue throughout the day.
Where is my game data stored?
Your in-game data (players, scores, settings) is stored in your browser's local storage on your device. It stays on your phone and does not automatically sync to other devices.
Do you use cookies or tracking?
The core game does not need cookies to work. If optional analytics or improvements are added in the future, you will see a clear notice before any non-essential tracking is used.

🎢 How to play Who Said Diz?

Who Said Diz turns wait times into game time with a fast, funny guessing game for the whole family.

How it works:

Start by adding 3+ players, picking your attraction, and answering a question in secret. Then guess who said what and place your bets. Get it right? Score points.

Play all day—from your first ride to your last—collecting attractions and lands along the way for bonus points.

Win by ending the day with the most points… and claim a prize the family agrees on (snack, souvenir, or bragging rights).

Legal: This independent product and its creators are not affiliated with, sponsored, authorized, or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company or any of its affiliates.

💰 How Scoring Works

Every player starts with 10 points. Points are won and lost each round based on your guess and wager.

✅
Correct guess
You earn your wager amount back. Wager 3, gain 3.
❌
Wrong guess
You lose your wager amount. Wager 3, lose 3.
✍️
Author bonus
If you wrote the selected answer, you earn +1 point for every player who guessed wrong.
🏠
House bonus
An optional bonus added by the host. Split evenly among correct guessers — only awarded if it divides evenly.
🛡️
Minimum score
No player can drop below 3 points, even after a bad round.

End-of-game bonuses are separate. Tap How are bonuses calculated? on the scores screen to learn more.

🎲 Wagers & House Bonus

Each round, you choose who wrote the answer and how many points to wager. Correct guesses win your wager back; wrong guesses lose it.

✅
Correct guess
You earn your wager amount. Wager 3, gain 3.
❌
Wrong guess
You lose your wager amount. Wager 3, lose 3.
👻
Ghost guess
Select Ghost if you think no one wrote this answer this round.
✍️
Author bonus
If you wrote the selected answer, you earn +1 point for every player who guessed wrong.
🏠
House bonus
An optional extra pot added by the host. Correct guessers split it evenly if it divides evenly.
