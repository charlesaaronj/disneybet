🎢 How to play Who Said Diz

Who Said Diz is a points game you play while waiting in line at Disney. The player with the most points at the end of the day wins a park snack paid for everyone else.

Each round:

  1. Choose the attraction you're waiting for.
  2. Everyone secretly answers a question about it.
  3. One answer is randomly selected.
  4. Everyone guesses who wrote it and wagers points.
  5. If you guess correctly, you gain the points you wagered. If you're wrong, you lose the points you wagered.
  6. The author earns +1 point for every player who wagered and guessed wrong.
  7. Guess correctly on an attraction round to collect it for end-of-day bonuses.

Points: Everyone starts with 10. Nobody goes below 3.

Bonuses are awarded at the end for attraction collection milestones.

Tiebreaker: Most correct guesses (wins) wins the tie.

🏅 End-of-day bonuses

You collect an attraction by guessing the correct author in a round tied to that attraction. Bonuses are awarded at the end of the game.

All bonuses use the same bonus point value you set at setup.

🗺️
Bonus A – Lands covered
Have at least one collected attraction in every land that appeared in the game.
⭐
Bonus B – Deep in a land
For each land where you have 2 or more different attractions collected, earn one bonus (one per qualifying land).
🏆
Bonus C – Top collector
Have the most total attractions collected overall. Ties all earn it.

Example: if bonus value = 2 and you earn bonus A + bonus C, you get +4 bonus points added to your final score.
